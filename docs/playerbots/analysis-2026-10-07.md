# 180-bot sim run: post-mortem (2026-10-07)

Source: read-only queries on `forever_sim_log`. Sample: 134,323 event rows (contiguous ids), 180 bots, 12,376 fights, window rows only (bots from the later restart, races 7/8, excluded). Nothing below was applied at the time of writing; R1-R5 are proposals.

## 1. Deaths
- Death rate per fight by level gap (mob minus bot): 0.5% at -1 or lower, 1.3% at 0, 5.5% at +1, about 25% at +2 or more (193 deaths in 770 fights). A gap of +1 or more is 79% of deaths.
- Mob-initiated fights kill 8.6% of the time (301 of 3,507) against 1.1% for bot-initiated (100 of 8,869). All bear deaths are mob-initiated: bots get aggroed.
- Creatures 1128 (L5-6 bear) and 1196 (bear) kill only dwarves, around (-5800,-300). Dwarves average 5.9 deaths per bot, tauren 0.09.
- Dwarves turned in only 4 distinct quests: they run out of quests, hub travel fails, they grind into bear ground (hypothesis).
- Flee does not work: the flee goal is 30-38 yd away and is reached in 5 s, the mob follows, the bot flees up to 3 times, then fights anyway (FIGHT_TOO_STRONG). After 1-2 flee attempts, 209 of 231 fights ended in death. Whether flee causes the deaths is untested.
- 1-5 deaths per bot cost no XP; only the 19 bots with 6 or more deaths fall behind.

## 2. Slow leveling
- Bot time: 53% moving outside combat, 29% combat, 16% idle (about 3% resting), 1% dead.
- About 1 kill per minute; 10.5, 22, 38 and 49 min to reach L2 to L5.
- 226 QUEST_HUB_TRAVEL events to hubs 2.5-13k yd away, often in the other faction's zone. 0 arrived: 172 NO_PATH, 42 PATH_PARTIAL_FAR, 11 UNREACHABLE.

## 3. Tick spikes
- 70 of 74 noncombat spikes follow a hub travel. Bots 324 and 61 had just run QUEST_NO_LOCAL then QUEST_HUB_TRAVEL to hub 362 (Tirisfal, about 8.5k yd), which failed about 1.7 s later. Attributed from timing; the log has no per-call timing.

## 4. NO_PROGRESS and UNREACHABLE_TARGET
- Almost all have tag `quest`. Top clusters: Durotar (-700,-4200; 52 rows), Teldrassil (10500,850; 49), Dun Morogh (47), map 2991 (46), Elwynn (-8900,-150; 37), Tirisfal (1900-2000,1500-1800; 36).
- At least 119 more NO_PROGRESS rows were suppressed by the per-login cap.

## 5. Trainers
- Races 95/96 had no trainer on map 2991 for warrior, hunter, rogue, mage (95) and shaman (96): 35 of 44 bots never trained. Dwarf shaman and undead paladin had no trainer. Effect on level is small.
- Since this run: trainers linked/added in the sim DB (see `sql/custom/world/2026_10_08_02_world_forever_sim_trainers.sql`, `2026_10_08_03_world_forever_undead_paladin_trainer.sql`).

## 6. Log completeness
- Rows are complete. Missing: quest_id on stuck rows, walk events for quest travel, path-search timing, idle reason.

## Proposed rules
- R1: Cache path failures for hubs; skip opposite-faction or unreachable hubs. Highest effect.
- R2: Cap grind targets at gap +1 or less, keep them near the start area.
- R3: Replace flee with aggro avoidance or fight-to-end, after an A/B run.
- R4: Trainers on map 2991 or drop the non-canonical combos (done in sim, see section 5).
- R5: Add the missing log fields.
