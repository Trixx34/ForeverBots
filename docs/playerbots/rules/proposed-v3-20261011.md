# Proposed bot rules v3 (2026-10-11)

Status: approved and implemented (see `../rules-v3-and-start-gear-20261011.md`); R-V3-02 navigation fixes for 400 and 2160 are still open. Source: `docs/playerbots/sim-run-800-analysis-run2-20261011.md` (800 bots, 3.9 h, old build). Sections referenced below are from that document.

## R-V3-01 Log the reason when a bot enters the grind task

- Condition: any switch to the `grind` activity.
- Action: add a reason code (level, prereq, path, hub, quarantine, blocked list, no quest) to the activity change and to the ledger. Logging only, no behavior change.
- Evidence: grind is 58% of ledger time (1,880 bot-hours) and hours with grind share above 0.87 earn 735 XP/h against 3,836 XP/h below 0.33 (section 1). The cause of grinding cannot be split from the current data (section 7).
- Expected effect: next run can say which cause to fix first.
- Risk: none beyond a slightly larger row.
- Confidence: high that it is needed.
- Rollback: remove the field.

## R-V3-02 Fix navigation-blocked quests in Dun Morogh and Durotar first

- Condition: quest blocked with `NO_PATH`, `UNREACHABLE` or `PATH_PARTIAL_FAR` for a bot in zone 1 or 14.
- Action: investigate and fix the nav data for quests 794, 5441, 400, 2160 (and 1656 in Mulgore); until fixed, put quests with a reward rate under 10% over at least 15 acceptances in the blocklist (candidates 794, 5441, 1656, 3905, 47).
- Evidence: races starting in those zones grind 74 to 76% of the time against 41 to 53% elsewhere; 794 rewarded 1 of 37, 5441 0 of 19, 1656 1 of 39, 3905 4 of 37, 47 2 of 36 (section 5).
- Expected effect: less grinding in the two worst start zones. Size unknown.
- Risk: blocklisting hides quests that could work after nav fixes.
- Confidence: medium. The link is correlational.
- Rollback: empty the blocklist entries.

## R-V3-03 Do not blocklist dangerous but completable quests

- Condition: quest with deaths but a reward rate above 25% (404, 112, 789, 792, 380, 488, 92544).
- Action: keep them off the blocklist; rely on group-after-deaths.
- Evidence: reward rates 27 to 74% (section 5), group-after-deaths mechanically works (section 3).
- Confidence: medium. Rollback: not applicable (no change).

## R-V3-04 Avoid Prideclaw in zone 16593 outside its quest

- Condition: bot level 5 to 6 in zone 16593, not on a quest that requires Prideclaw (92515, 92553), grinding or on a profession or vendor trip.
- Action: treat entry 251245 as a hostile to path around (aggro radius margin) and exclude it from grind targets.
- Evidence: 398 deaths, 90 bots, 36% of zone deaths; 232 of 398 on grind or trips (section 2).
- Expected effect: up to about 20% fewer deaths in that zone (upper bound, hypothesis; no with/without comparison).
- Risk: longer paths, extra stuck cases.
- Confidence: low to medium.
- Rollback: remove the exclusion.

## R-V3-05 Blocklist candidates in zone 16593 with zero rewards

- Condition: quests 92515, 92553, 92516, 92551 for bots below level 7.
- Action: add to the blocklist (or require a group) after approval.
- Evidence: 0 rewards, 155 deaths from 62 bots; 92515 and 92553 had 16 and 35 acceptances in total, 92516 and 92551 only 10 and 5 (below the minimum of 15, so those two are weak evidence) (section 2).
- Expected effect: at most about 14% of zone deaths.
- Risk: loses the quest chain if these quests become winnable at a higher level.
- Confidence: medium for 92515 and 92553, low for the others.
- Rollback: remove from the blocklist.

## R-V3-06 Do not use `no_kill` for kill statistics until fixed

- Condition: analysis and any rule reading `COMBAT_SUMMARY.outcome`.
- Action: credit `kills` for kills by pets, damage over time and non-last-hit kills, or add a kill credit source field.
- Evidence: 0 of 1,057 `no_kill` rows have kills above 0, yet 86% coincide with a kill-XP event (control 13 to 15%) (section 4).
- Confidence: high that the label is wrong, cause unverified.
- Rollback: revert the counter change.
