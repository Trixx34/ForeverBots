# Proposed rules v1 (2026-10-07)

All entries are PROPOSED. None is approved; none has been adopted yet. Evidence is in `docs/playerbots/sim-run-analysis-20261007.md`.

## R-01 Death-loop breaker (v1)
- Condition: a bot dies N times (provisional N=3) within 5 min and 150 yd.
- Action: avoid that area or killer entry for that bot for 30 min and choose another target.
- Evidence: 53% of 5,005 deaths in R3 are repeat deaths in that window; 37 bots (15%) hold 64% of deaths.
- Expected effect: fewer deaths and less time lost; deaths per hour should stop rising after hour 3.
- Risk: N and the radius are guesses; graveyard proximity makes the window partly trivial. Tune from the chain-length distribution first.
- Confidence: high for the pattern, medium for the fix.
- Rollback: config flag off or N very large.

## R-02 Avoid list (v1)
- Condition: target entry 250927 (map 2991 only), 822 or 116.
- Action: never pick as a grind target; widen aggro avoidance around them. Prowler 118 after more data. Narg 79 not added (16 fights).
- Evidence: 250927 98% over 453 fights (R2 99% over 94); 822 64% over 647 (R2 51% over 195); 116 57% over 448 (R2 64% over 327).
- Expected effect: removes a large share of deaths in those zones.
- Risk: these may be kill-quest objectives (not checked), which would quarantine quests and add stuck events; bot level is a confounder.
- Confidence: high for 250927, medium for 822 and 116.
- Rollback: remove the entry.

## R-03 Aggro avoidance in idle and grind states (v1)
- Condition: bot idle or selecting a grind target.
- Action: apply the same path and target avoidance as quest travel.
- Evidence: 25.5k of 29.9k mob-first fights start with no activity; no baseline share of all fights yet.
- Expected effect: fewer mob-first fights.
- Risk: more NO_GRIND_TARGET stalls.
- Confidence: low (hypothesis).
- Rollback: config flag off.

## R-04 Hunter ammo and Auto Shot (v1)
- Condition: hunter bot.
- Action: provision ammo and a ranged weapon at creation; use Auto Shot as the main attack.
- Evidence: Raptor Strike 12,210 casts versus Auto Shot 2,286; NO_AMMO present.
- Expected effect: hunters fight at range.
- Risk: provisioning must match the item data.
- Confidence: medium.
- Rollback: revert provisioning.

## R-05 Judgement (v1)
- Condition: paladin with an active seal.
- Action: first verify the running worldserver process start time against the build time and that the executable comes from this tree; then check at runtime whether the aura state is set. If unresolved, remove Judgement from the rotation.
- Evidence: zero Judgement successes, failures with CASTER_AURASTATE.
- Expected effect: removes wasted casts or enables Judgement.
- Risk: lower paladin damage if removed.
- Confidence: high that it is broken, cause unknown.
- Rollback: re-add to the rotation.

## R-06 Wand failures (v1)
- Condition: Shoot fails with DONT_REPORT.
- Action: do not count it toward melee fallback.
- Evidence: fallback fired 5 times; DONT_REPORT looks benign.
- Expected effect: negligible.
- Risk: none known.
- Confidence: low importance.
- Rollback: restore the counting.

## R-07 Flee A/B at mode 1 (v1)
