# Proposed rules v2 (2026-10-11)

All entries are PROPOSED. None is approved; none has been adopted. Evidence is in `docs/playerbots/sim-run-800-analysis-20261011.md` (800-bot run, 2026-10-08 20:10 to 2026-10-09 18:32, sim DB `forever_sim_log.bot_event`, id >= 9815501; the sim data will be deleted, numbers are copied into the analysis).

## Status of the entries (2026-10-11)
R-V2-01, R-V2-02 and R-V2-03 are implemented in code, each behind a setting in `botserver.conf.dist` (see `docs/playerbots/loop-guards-20261011.md`); they are still unmeasured, the next sim run decides whether they stay. R-V2-04 to R-V2-08 are not implemented; R-V2-04 only got its evidence rows (`VENDOR_RESULT`).

## R-V2-01 Re-equip combat weapon after profession tool use (v1)
- Condition: a bot with a weapon-using class (warrior, paladin, hunter, rogue) has a profession tool (items 7005 Skinning Knife, 2901 Mining Pick, 6256 Fishing Pole) in the main hand, or a hunter has no ammo.
- Action: swap back the combat weapon right after the tool action; for hunters, provision ammo and restock at the vendor; if no usable weapon, do not start fights and fix it first.
- Evidence: `CAST_SKIPPED_WEAPON` 20,409 rows (mainhand 7005 in 18,943), `CAST_FAILED NO_AMMO` 10,894 rows (52 hunters); 100 bots with >=300 `APPROACH_TIMEOUT` are all classes 1-4; total `APPROACH_TIMEOUT` 139,902 rows, 126,585 from 82 bots, 1,279 bot-hours (7%). Weapon-class bots with the loop reached avg L7.0 versus 8.7 for weapon-class bots without it (n=100 vs 94; 161 other weapon-class bots with no timeouts averaged L5.9, so the picture is mixed). Item class of the tool ids not verified in the sim data.
- Expected effect: removes about 7% of bot-hours of standing idle, fixes about 12% of bots.
- Risk: items may be equipped intentionally for skill-up; verify item ids first.
- Confidence: high for the pattern, medium for the cause.
- Rollback: revert the equip logic; flag off.

## R-V2-02 Approach timeout escalation (v1)
- Condition: `APPROACH_TIMEOUT` for the same target or the same bot more than 5 times in 10 min with `moving=false`, `los=true`.
- Action: stop retrying; mark the cause (weapon, ammo, power) and fix it or log out and in; never re-pick the same target for 10 min.
- Evidence: 82 bots produce 126,585 of 139,902 rows, about 200 per bot per 2 h (one every ~36 s), for hours.
- Expected effect: bots leave the loop; fewer log rows (about 1.6% of volume).
- Risk: hides a deeper bug if the cause is not logged.
- Confidence: high.
- Rollback: threshold very large.

## R-V2-03 Profession and workshop trips: distance, level and loop guard (v1)
- Condition: `PROF_TRIP` or `WORKSHOP_TRIP` planned.
- Action: only same-zone trainers within 500 yd, and only if every mob on the path is at most bot level + 2; do not re-issue the same trip within 30 min after it was dropped; require an arrival or failure event; cap trainer cycling (5 trainers tried per skill, then give up for 6 h).
- Evidence: `PROF_TRIP` 71,449 rows, avg 1,426 yd, 98% `have=false`, dropped about 3 s later in the sampled bots (bot 144: 543 trips in 22 h across 5 trainers); `WORKSHOP_TRIP` 153,971 rows, 334 bots, 47 bots with >=100/h; example death chain: bot 64 L5 went Elwynn to Duskwood on a Mining trainer trip, then died to L20-24 mobs; Duskwood 2,233 deaths from 24 human bots (killers 930, 202 average level 23-24 vs bot 7), 6,186 AGGRO_BLOCKED rows there.
- Expected effect: removes 6% of bot-hours of trips/loops and about 10% of deaths (zone 10 plus other >=+6 cases 2,759).
- Risk: bots learn professions later; some professions need distant trainers.
- Confidence: medium (loop observed; Duskwood cause shown for one bot, others not traced).
- Rollback: flag off.

## R-V2-04 Free bag slots before quest turn-in and vendor loops (v1)
- Condition: free slots = 0.
- Action: sell grey items and trash first (or destroy), then turn in; do not plan another `VENDOR_TRIP` within 5 min if the last one did not free a slot; log the sell result.
- Evidence: `VENDOR_TRIP` with 0 free slots 14,421 rows (518 bots) + 2,041 forced (159 bots); 180-240 distinct bots with full bags every hour from 22:00; sample bot 16 repeats "turn in Supplies to Tannok", vendor at 3 yd, grind every 5 min for the whole hour without a reward; `QUEST_TURNIN_PLAN` 14,470 vs `QUEST_REWARDED` 8,169; BAG_FULL/QUEST_LOG_FULL in quest_blocked.
- Expected effect: unblocks quests for about a quarter of the bots.
- Risk: selling needed items; sell only quality poor/white non-quest items.
- Confidence: medium (cause of zero-slot persistence not verified).
- Rollback: flag off.

## R-V2-05 Death-loop breaker, stronger than R-01 (v2)
- Condition: bot dies 3 times to the same killer entry or in the same 150 yd within 30 min.
- Action: blocklist that killer entry and area for that bot for 2 h and move to a lower-level grind area or quest hub; add the entry to a shared avoid list after 10 bots do the same.
- Evidence: 25,406 deaths, 107 bots (14%) hold 53%; 115 bot/killer pairs with >30 deaths hold 7,237 deaths; bot 527 died 461 times to Ornery Galestrider (251707); death chain >=3 is 5,141 deaths (20%). Top killers by shared entry: 251245 (1,830 deaths, 129 bots), 251707 (1,580, 47), 3112 (928, 89), 3111 (748, 70), 822 (719, 62), 251291 (689, 69).
- Expected effect: cuts deaths by about 30-50% and the 20-30% of time those bots lose.
- Risk: blocklisted mobs may be quest targets; may increase NO_GRIND_TARGET.
- Confidence: high for the pattern, medium for the effect size.
- Rollback: config flag off or threshold large.

## R-V2-06 Flee only with an escape, otherwise fight or kite (v1)
- Condition: bot flees (`combat_flee`, `combat_flee_low_hp`) while the mob is in melee range and still attacking.
- Action: if the mob is the same speed, stop fleeing after the first failed attempt (already `FLEE_GAVE_UP`, 3,191) and use defensive cooldown, bandage, or spell; consider fighting back earlier rather than at 15% health.
- Evidence: 11,655 of 25,406 deaths (46%) happen with a flee goto active; 6,687 + 4,965 of last decisions are flee; bots dealt 16 hp (mob-started) in the last 10 s but took about 35-41; `FLEE_LEVEL_DIFF` 112,973 and `FLEE_ENDED` 79,320 rows. Death rate per pick rises from 4.0% (diff 0) to 9.9% (+1) and 21.6% (+2) (n=85,106; 43,015; 17,598).
- Expected effect: fewer deaths while fleeing; unknown magnitude.
- Risk: more deaths at +2 and above if the cap is relaxed; combine with a target cap of bot level + 1.
- Confidence: low (hypothesis, no controlled comparison).
- Rollback: revert.

## R-V2-07 Target selection: cap at bot level + 1 and skip grey (v1)
- Condition: grind target selection.
- Action: prefer mobs of level bot-1 to bot+1; skip mobs 4 or more levels below once XP/kill falls below 25 and a better candidate exists within 300 yd.
- Evidence: kills at -5 give 13.8 xp (23,552), -4 24.7 (30,547) versus 70.3 at 0 (40,228) and 71.0 at +1 (20,997); death rate per pick rises from 1.0% (diff <=-3, n=156,295) to 4.0% at 0 and 9.9% at +1; XP per kill is flat at 44-52 for L1-L12; 22% of kills are at -4 or below.
- Expected effect: roughly +20-30% XP per kill at a small increase in deaths (the net is unknown).
- Risk: deaths at +1 are 2.5x those at 0, so the cap may need to stay at 0 for weak classes (warriors/hunters/rogues/warlocks die 40-51 per bot).
- Confidence: low-medium; needs an A/B in the sim.
- Rollback: restore the previous target filter.

## R-V2-08 Quest quarantine and level gate (v1)
- Condition: a quest fails 3 times (`QUEST_QUARANTINED`, 5,108 rows) or 10 times across bots (`QUEST_QUARANTINED_GLOBAL`, 1,465 rows).
- Action: shorten the global skip from 1 h to be re-checked only after a code or navmesh change; pre-filter quests of known blocked ids (92470, 789, 92515, 92553, 792, 218, 92544, 2160, 93319, 92516, 182, 5441, 60, 93951) and objective type 14 (`OBJECTIVE_UNSUPPORTED`); do not offer a quest where quest min level exceeds bot level by 1 (`QUEST_LEVEL` 8,199 rows, e.g. 6394, 96638, 92471).
- Evidence: top quests above have 296-432 blocked rows and 107-195 bots each; `NO_PATH`/`UNREACHABLE`/`PATH_PARTIAL_FAR` 14,651 rows; `QUEST_PREREQ` 8,050 rows; `QUEST_HUB_NONE` 2,323 rows (bots fall to grind).
- Expected effect: fewer wasted trips (14.1M yd of quest walking in total); more bots keep quests longer.
- Risk: a pre-filter hides navmesh bugs that should be fixed (bot-nav); this is a stop-gap.
- Confidence: medium.
- Rollback: empty the filter.
