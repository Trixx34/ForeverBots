## 1. Headline

Slow progress is not mainly deaths or XP rate. It is that productive bots collapse over time: bots with at least one XP event per hour fall from 750 (22:00) to 215 (17:00 next day), and about 587 of 800 bots earned no XP in the 16:00-17:00 hour. They sit in decision loops that never finish. Observed loops (all directly visible in the log, impact in bot-hours is a lower bound):

| Loop | Evidence | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| Profession-trainer trip planned, dropped 3 s later, repeated cycling through trainers 1200-3000 yd away | `PROF_TRIP` 71,449 rows, 94 bots with >=20/h in 778 bot-hours; 98% `have=false`; each followed by `QUEST_GRIND` within 3 s | 4% of bot-hours; some trips are real and walk L5 bots into Duskwood (see 3) |
| Cooking workshop trip ("spell focus 4", 16-190 yd) re-issued every ~17 s, never reached | `WORKSHOP_TRIP` 153,971 rows, 334 bots, rate 0 to 11k/h over the run; 47 bots with >=100/h in 367 bot-hours | 2% of bot-hours, growing |
| `APPROACH_TIMEOUT` "cannot reach X within 15 s" with mob at 4-15 yd, `los=true`, `moving=false` | 139,902 rows; 107 bots in 1,279 bot-hours; the 100 bots with >=300 are all warriors, paladins, hunters, rogues (32/11/28/29 bots) | 7% of bot-hours; 126,585 of the rows come from 82 bots |
| Full bags: `VENDOR_TRIP` with 0 free slots every 5 min, and turn-in plan then vendor then grind with the quest never rewarded | `VENDOR_TRIP` free_slots=0: 14,421 rows from 518 bots (+2,041 forced); ~190-200 bots per hour have full bags from 22:00 on; hourly rows with 0 slots 51 to 967 | ~25% of bots at any time |
| Quest loot spawns unusable ("no path") tried one after another | `QUEST_LOOT_SPAWN_EMPTY` 67,124; 3k per hour constant | steady drain |
| No quests left: `QUEST_GRIND` 227k rows (332/h to 14k/h), `QUEST_WALK_START` 25k/h to 1.7k/h | quest_blocked, see 5 | |

Root cause of the weapon-class group (hypothesis from item ids, item table not available in sim hotfixes DB): `CAST_SKIPPED_WEAPON` shows `mainhand` 7005 (Skinning Knife) 19k times, 2901 (Mining Pick) 1.2k, 6256 (Fishing Pole). ## 7. What the data could not answer

- Deaths per bot per quest: death events have `quest_id` NULL. Ask plumber to log the active quest/task (quest_id and task: quest, grind, trip, flee) on death and `COMBAT_START`.
- No per-state time ledger (walking, idle, waiting, in loop); proposed: `ai_stats` could add seconds per top-level activity.
- `COMBAT_SUMMARY.outcome`: `no_kill` 238,920 vs actual kill XP events 245,084 and `partial_kills` 10,793 (inconsistent; `no_kill` looks mis-set at summary time). Do not use the outcome for kill rates until fixed.
- Did `PROF_TRIP` walks actually walk (no GOTO_START with a profession tag, so unclear whether the move is issued and cancelled or never issued). The Duskwood death chain shows at least some trips are real.
- Why `WORKSHOP_TRIP` never ends (no arrival event, no failure reason), and why the 0-free-slot vendor trips do not free slots (no sell/repair result event). Item class of 7005/2901/6256 not verified (sim hotfixes DB has no item rows).
- Win versus loss comparisons per class need target level in `COMBAT_SUMMARY`.
- Aura/cast detail exists but was not mined here.
